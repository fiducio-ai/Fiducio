License 1.1. Their copyright notices and full licenses are included alongside
the WOFF2 files. The Apache-2.0 license for Fiducio code does not replace these
font licenses.

License sources (Google Fonts mirrors of the font projects):
- https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/newsreader/OFL.txt
- https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ibmplexsans/OFL.txt
- https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ibmplexmono/OFL.txt

The WOFF2 files are retained unchanged from the project-page PR. Their embedded
name tables identify the respective font families and SIL OFL licensing.
